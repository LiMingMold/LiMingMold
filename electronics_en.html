DFM / Tooling / Injection Molding

Problems We Help Solve

  • DFM review before tooling
    Review wall thickness, shrinkage, warpage, draft angles, snap-fit strength, bosses, assembly interference, and production risks.
  • Integrated tooling and molding
    In-house tooling and molding integration helps shorten trial, mold modification, and production launch communication.
  • Engineering plastic molding
    Discuss material conditions such as heat resistance, flame retardancy, impact resistance, dimensional stability, appearance, and insulation.
  • Insert molding and post-processing
    Support metal inserts, nut inserts, heat staking, printing, laser marking, assembly, testing, and packaging.
  • Flexible production
    Suitable for multiple models, different versions, development projects, second-source evaluation, and Taiwan-based supply chain backup.

Materials

Materials & Application Conditions

Based on customer requirements, we can support engineering plastics such as PC, PC+ABS, ABS, PBT, PA, PPS, LCP, and TPE, and discuss flame retardancy, heat resistance, dimensional stability, impact resistance, appearance, and insulation requirements. If UL94 V-0 or other flame-retardant grades are required, we can assist with material selection and evaluation according to customer specifications.
